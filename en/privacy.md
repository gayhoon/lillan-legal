# Lillan Privacy Policy

[한국어](../privacy) · English

Effective: 13 November 2026

Lillan ("the app") is an app where parents log their baby's day together. It is made and run by an individual developer in the Republic of Korea. You can reach us at neovianhoon@gmail.com.

## 1. Information we collect

| Category | What | What it is used for |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email address, sign-in identifiers (Apple, Google, Kakao), and the name, nickname and profile photo received from Google or Kakao | Signing in and confirming your account |
| Baby details | Name, birthday, feeding method, and the choices you make for the baby picture (hair, skin, eye and clothing colors) | The log, age-based guidance, and showing the baby picture |
| Log | Feeds (amount, method), sleep, diapers, temperature, notes, vaccine checks, weight, height and head size with the date measured, and who logged each entry | Saving the log, stats, and sharing it with your partner |
| Family | Role (Mom, Dad and so on), display name, invite code | Logging together |
| Photos and videos | Reduced copies of the baby photos and videos you choose (including video sound), the day they are placed on, the time taken, captions, and who added them | The calendar and album, shared with your family |
| Feedback | What you send, a random device identifier, the screen you were on, device and OS, language, app version | Reading and answering feedback |
| Parenting help usage | The number of questions per account per day | Managing usage limits |
| Notifications | Push notification token (a value that points to the device that receives notifications), notification settings, app language | Keeping the widget up to date when your partner logs, and sending notifications if you turn them on |

We do not collect advertising identifiers, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps and websites.

## 2. Your baby's information

The app is not meant to be used by children. A parent or legal guardian enters the baby's information. Temperature, weight, height, head size, feeding and sleep entries can relate to health. The app stores this information only when a parent enters it, and uses it only for the log and stats, sharing within the family, and the AI parenting help if the parent has agreed to it. Weight, height and head size are not sent to the AI. You can edit or delete what you entered in the app at any time.

The baby picture is a drawing built into the app, colored with the choices you make. It does not use photos of your baby. Your choices are used only so that your family sees the same picture. They are not used for stats, guidance or the AI, and are not sent to other companies.

Only the photos and videos a parent chooses are uploaded. Before upload, the device reduces their size and removes capture details, including location. Only the date and time taken are stored separately. Photos and videos can be seen only by members of your family. They are not used for stats, guidance or the AI, we do not run face recognition or automatic sorting on them, and they are not sent to other companies. Lillan does not keep the originals, so please keep them on your device.

## 3. AI parenting help

The parenting help tab can be used only after you agree. When you send a question, the information below is sent to Anthropic and used to write the answer.

- Always: your question, the earlier conversation, your baby's age, and the feeding method
- If you turn on "Include recent entries": a numeric summary of feeds, sleep, diapers and temperature for the last 7 days
- Never: your baby's name, the notes in your log, your email address

Lillan's server does not store questions or answers. It stores only the number of questions per day. The conversation stays on your device, and you can clear it on the parenting help screen. If you report an answer, that question and answer are sent to us as feedback and kept for the same period as feedback. If you withdraw your consent from the parenting help menu, nothing more is sent and the conversation on that device is deleted. All other features work without parenting help.

## 4. Keeping and deleting

- We keep your information until you delete your account.
- You can delete it at any time in the app under Settings › Logging together › Delete account. To ask without the app, use the [deletion page](https://gayhoon.github.io/lillan-legal/en/delete).
- When you delete a photo or video, it disappears from everyone's album in your family and the file on the server is deleted too (within 3 days at the latest).
- When you delete your account, we delete your account and member details. If you are the last member of the family, we also delete the baby details, the log, vaccine checks, photos and videos.
- If your partner stays in the family, the baby details, log, photos and videos stay for your partner. Only the "logged by" mark is removed from the entries you made.
- Parenting help usage is deleted when you delete your account.
- The push notification token is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
- Feedback sent from the app is tied to a device identifier, so deleting your account does not delete it. We delete it one year after we receive it. Contact us if you want it deleted sooner.

## 5. Companies that process data for us

| Company | What it does | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase Inc. | Stores accounts, the log, photos and videos | Data is stored in the Republic of Korea (Seoul region) |
| Google LLC | Sends sign-in code emails, Google sign-in, delivers push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) | United States and other countries |
| Apple Inc. | Sign in with Apple, delivers push notifications | United States and other countries |
| Kakao Corp. | Kakao sign-in | Republic of Korea |
| Anthropic PBC ([anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com)) | Writes the answers for AI parenting help | United States and other countries |

Wherever you live, your account, log, photos and videos are stored on servers in the Republic of Korea, and the companies above process the data described here in their own locations.

To sign you in, your email address and sign-in identifier are sent over the network to the companies above. This happens when you request a sign-in code or use that sign-in method, and each company keeps the data for as long as its own policy requires. Sign-in code emails are sent through Google's mail service.

If you use AI parenting help, the information in section 3 is sent over the network to Anthropic each time you send a question. Anthropic keeps it for a set period under its own policy. Nothing is sent if you withdraw your consent or do not use parenting help.

When your partner logs an entry or adds photos, the push notification token and the notification text are sent to Apple's or Google's notification service and delivered to your device. The notification text does not include your baby's name, feed amounts, temperature or notes, and for photos it includes only how many were added (not the photos, captions or dates). Feed and sleep reminders are created on your device only and are not sent to the server.

## 6. Who else can see the data

A person who joins your family with an invite code can see, edit and delete that family's baby details, log, photos and videos. We do not provide the data to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## 7. Your rights

You can see, correct and delete your information in the app. For anything else, such as asking us to stop processing or to send you a copy of your data, write to neovianhoon@gmail.com. A legal guardian can use the same rights for a child's information. You can also contact the privacy regulator where you live.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and the server allows only members of a family to access that family's data. Photos and videos are kept in encrypted storage and are served only to requests confirmed to come from a family member. There is a limit on how much one family can upload.

## 9. Health information

The app's stats and guidance, and the answers from AI parenting help, are not a diagnosis or treatment and can be wrong. If your baby seems different from usual or you are worried, talk to a doctor or nurse.

## 10. Contact and changes

Privacy contact: Lillan privacy, neovianhoon@gmail.com. If this policy changes, we will say so on this page and in the app before the change takes effect.
