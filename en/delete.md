# Deleting your Lillan account and data

[한국어](../delete) · English

How to delete your Lillan account and the data linked to it.

## Delete in the app

1. Open the Lillan app.
2. Go to Settings › Logging together.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Ask without the app

If you have removed the app or cannot open it, email neovianhoon@gmail.com with the email address you used to sign in and the words "Delete account". We will delete it within 7 days of your request and let you know.

## What is deleted

- Your account and member details
- If you are the last member of the family: the baby details, the log, vaccine checks, photos and videos
- Parenting help usage, and the parenting help conversation on that device

## What stays

- If your partner stays in the family, the baby details, log, photos and videos stay for your partner. Only the "logged by" mark is removed from the entries you made.
- Feedback sent from the app is kept for one year from the day we receive it. Contact us if you want it deleted sooner.
